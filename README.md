**Project Summary:**
This project seeks to identify a few indicators of heavy traffic such as weather type and time of day. The goal of this project was to create “exploratory” graphs: graphs that deepen our understanding of the data. 

**Key Findings:**

Three time indicators of traffic volume:
* Warmer months (March-October) typically have more cars on the road than the colder month (November-February) per day at any given time
* Traffic is higher during the weekdays (Monday-Friday) and lesser on the weekends
* During the week, most cars are on the road from 7-8 a.m. and 4-5 p.m. while weekend traffic is more stagnant

Three weather condition indicators of traffic volume:
* Light rain and snow
* Proximity thunderstorm with drizzle
* Shower snow

**Datasource:**
The dataset we will be using for this project is from the [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/492/metro+interstate+traffic+volume) donated by John Hogue. The dataset was collected between Minneapolis and Saint Paul for westbound traffic on I-94. There are 48,204 rows and 9 columns in the dataset. For further exploration of the columns, please reference the project.
